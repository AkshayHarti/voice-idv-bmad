Technique 01 · First Principles Thinking — the excavation

Strip the screen to bedrock.

We bulldozed every pixel and asked: what must the user know, at this exact moment, or the whole ritual fails? Click each stratum to dig deeper.

topsoil · −0m · assumptions

“Show them everything, just in case.”

Rejected. Chrome is sediment, not structure. Dig.

clay · −3m · bedrock truth ①

What do I do right now?

The user needs to know what to do at that moment. One current action. Nothing else earns the light.

rock · −9m · bedrock truth ②

Where am I in the ritual?

The user needs to know where he is at in the process — position exists so failure is locatable: you know exactly which parts you could not complete.

★ bedrock · −20m · bedrock truth ③

Proven or merely attempted?

Not done-vs-failed. Proven-vs-attempted. A step can be tried and still unproven — and the screen must say so honestly.

the three truths · i
Current action

One verb, now. “Hold your ID in the frame.”

the three truths · ii
Position

You are at step 3 of 5. Failure has an address.

the three truths · iii
Proof state

Proven ✓ vs attempted ○. Never blur them.

⚖️ Rebuild verdict: every current screen element must re-earn its place under the three truths — or be buried in the dig.

“Position exists so failure is locatable.” — the line that survived the excavation.

Technique 03 · Job to Be Done — the patient hunter

The app idles. Then it pounces.

We stopped asking “what should the screen show?” and asked “what job hired this screen?” Answer: a hunter that waits in the grass until conditions are processing-ready — then auto-captures. No shutter button. No mercy for manual.

IDLE · app waits — “app idles until a document appears…”
🪪 DOC JOB · “…then auto-captures when conditions are processing-ready”
🤳 SELFIE JOB · “selfie auto-captures once the natural face is processing-ready”
✦ CONFIRM JOB · post-capture confirmation offers retry — the safety net under the pounce
AUTO-CAPTURE ALWAYS · never manual

facilitator decision — the shutter button was voted off the island

🚶✨
The confirmation job, in one line: the user walks away believing the capture succeeded. Not hoping. Believing. That feeling is the deliverable.
